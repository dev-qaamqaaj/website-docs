OBD and the Client are each a "Party" and together the "Parties". The Parties agree as follows.

Definitions

  1. "Website" means the website OBD has designed and developed for the Client from the documents and content the Client supplied, including any agreed revisions.
  2. "Services" means the development, deployment and, where applicable, support and maintenance services described in Schedule A.
  3. "Package" means the deployment package the Client selects in Schedule B: Option A ("Complete Care") or Option B ("Launch Essentials").
  4. "Third-Party Services" means the domain name, web hosting, email and any other service supplied by an external provider.
  5. "Go-Live" means the date the Website is first publicly accessible on the Client's domain.
  6. "Care Period" means the period from Go-Live until {{ c.project.support_until }} (Complete Care only).
  7. "Minor Change" has the meaning given in Schedule C.
  8. "Business Day" means Monday to Saturday, excluding public holidays in Pakistan.

Engagement and scope

  1. The Client engages OBD to provide the Services, and OBD agrees to provide them, on the terms of this Agreement.
  2. The Services are limited to those described in Schedule A for the selected Package. Anything not expressly included is out of scope and will be handled under Clause 8 (Changes).
  3. OBD confirms that development of the Website has been completed and a preview has been shared with the Client.

Term

  1. This Agreement starts on the Effective Date. It continues until the later of Go-Live and the end of the Care Period (if any), unless terminated earlier under Clause 15.

Fees and payment

  1. The Client shall pay the fees set out in Schedule B (the "Fees") according to the payment schedule in Schedule B. All amounts are in Pakistani Rupees ({{ cur }}).
  2. The Fees are referral rates, below OBD's standard rates, and apply to this engagement only.
  3. OBD will issue an invoice or payment receipt for every payment received.
  4. If any instalment remains unpaid 7 days after its due date, OBD may pause work until payment is received. Agreed timelines will be extended by the length of the pause.
  5. The Fees do not include the cost of Third-Party Services (Clause 5) or out-of-scope work (Clause 8).

Third-Party Services, ownership of accounts

  1. The Client will purchase the domain, hosting and any email plan directly from the provider, in the Client's own name, with OBD's guidance. OBD will not charge any commission, markup or fee on Third-Party Services.
  2. Indicative annual costs are shown in Schedule B for information only. Actual prices are set by the provider and may change.
  3. The Client owns these accounts and is responsible for paying for and renewing them. OBD is not responsible for loss of service caused by a missed renewal, provider outage, provider policy or provider price change.
  4. The Client will give OBD the access needed to deploy and configure the Website (for example, delegated or temporary access). The Client may revoke that access at any time after Go-Live.

Client responsibilities

  1. The Client will provide accurate information, content, approvals and access promptly. Delays on the Client's side extend the timeline accordingly.
  2. The Client confirms that it owns, or has permission to use, all content, images, logos and trademarks it supplies, and that such content is lawful.
  3. The Client will nominate one point of contact who can give approvals on its behalf.

Timeline and acceptance

  1. OBD aims to complete deployment within five (5) Business Days of the Third-Party Services being purchased and access being provided. This is a target, not a guaranteed deadline.
  2. At Go-Live, OBD will walk the Client through the Website and hand over all credentials. The Client will have three (3) Business Days to report any defect in writing.
  3. The Website is accepted when the Client signs the Go-Live Acceptance & Handover certificate, or when three (3) Business Days pass without a defect being reported, whichever comes first. Acceptance does not affect OBD's obligations during the launch stabilisation or Care Period.

Changes

  1. Under Complete Care, Minor Changes are free of charge for the first {{ c.project.free_changes_window_days }} days after Go-Live, as described in Schedule C.
  2. Any other change, including new pages, new features, redesigns, integrations or major content changes, is a Change Request. OBD will quote the cost and timeline in writing (including by WhatsApp or email), and will begin work only once the Client approves the quote.

Support and maintenance

  1. Under Complete Care, OBD will provide support and maintenance during the Care Period as described in Schedule C.
  2. Under Launch Essentials, OBD will fix defects in its own work reported within {{ c.project.launch_warranty_days }} days of Go-Live. Any support after that can be arranged separately at OBD's standard rates.

Intellectual property

  1. Once all Fees due have been paid in full, ownership of the Website's custom design, code written specifically for the Client, and all Client-supplied content passes to the Client.
  2. OBD keeps ownership of its pre-existing tools, templates, components, methods and know-how, and grants the Client a perpetual, non-exclusive, royalty-free licence to use them as part of the Website.
  3. Open-source and third-party components remain subject to their own licences.
  4. OBD may mention the Client's name and show the Website in its portfolio unless the Client opts out here:  Client opts out

Confidentiality and credentials

  1. Each Party will keep the other's confidential information (including business information, pricing and account credentials) confidential and use it only for this Agreement. This obligation survives for two (2) years after the Agreement ends.
  2. OBD will store Client credentials securely, share them only with personnel working on the project, and hand them over in full at Go-Live. OBD recommends that the Client changes all passwords after handover.
  3. OBD will process the Client's personal data only to deliver the Services and meet legal, tax and record-keeping obligations.

Warranties

  1. OBD will perform the Services with reasonable skill and care, in line with good industry practice.
  2. OBD does not guarantee search-engine rankings, traffic, sales or leads, or the uninterrupted availability of Third-Party Services.

Limitation of liability

  1. OBD's total liability under this Agreement is limited to the total Fees actually paid by the Client under it.
  2. Neither Party is liable for indirect or consequential loss, including loss of profit, revenue, data or goodwill, except where caused by fraud or wilful misconduct.

Taxes and records

  1. Each Party is responsible for its own taxes under the laws of Pakistan. If the Client is required by law to withhold tax from any payment, the Client will provide OBD with the relevant tax deduction certificate or challan within fifteen (15) days of payment.
  2. The Client will provide its CNIC and, if registered, NTN/STRN so OBD can issue correct invoices and keep proper records.
  3. Both Parties will keep a signed copy of this Agreement, the invoices and the payment receipts as evidence of the transaction.

Termination

  1. Either Party may terminate this Agreement by giving fourteen (14) days' written notice.
  2. Either Party may terminate immediately by written notice if the other materially breaches this Agreement and does not remedy the breach within seven (7) days of being notified.
  3. On termination, the Client will pay for all Services performed up to the termination date. The development fee covers work already completed and delivered, and is non-refundable. Fees paid for completed milestones are non-refundable.
  4. On termination, OBD will hand over all credentials and a copy of the Website files, provided all Fees due have been paid.

Force majeure

  1. Neither Party is liable for delay or failure caused by events beyond its reasonable control, including natural disasters, power or internet outages, provider failures, government action or civil unrest. Affected timelines are extended accordingly.

Governing law and disputes

  1. This Agreement is governed by the laws of the Islamic Republic of Pakistan.
  2. The Parties will first try to resolve any dispute in good faith through discussion between the Client and {{ c.company.founder }} for fifteen (15) days.
  3. Any dispute not resolved in that time will be referred to arbitration by a sole arbitrator appointed by mutual agreement under the Arbitration Act, 1940, seated at {{ m.val(city, "30mm") }}. Subject to that, the courts at {{ m.val(city, "30mm") }} have exclusive jurisdiction.

General

  1. Entire agreement. This Agreement, including its Schedules, is the entire agreement between the Parties on its subject matter and replaces all earlier discussions, messages and proposals about it.
  2. Amendments. Changes to this Agreement must be agreed in writing. A clear written confirmation from both Parties by email or WhatsApp is sufficient for Change Requests and scheduling.
  3. Notices. Notices may be given by email or WhatsApp to the contact details in this Agreement.
  4. Electronic signature. This Agreement may be signed electronically and in counterparts, in accordance with the Electronic Transactions Ordinance, 2002. Each signed copy is an original.
  5. Independent parties. OBD is an independent contractor. Nothing in this Agreement creates a partnership, employment or agency relationship.
  6. Severability. If any provision is found unenforceable, the rest of the Agreement remains in force.

SCHEDULE CSupport, Maintenance & Minor Changes

This Schedule applies to Option A: Complete Care, for the Care Period ending {{ c.project.support_until }}.

Support & maintenance includes

  • Fixing defects in OBD's work
  • Keeping the website online after hosting, DNS or SSL configuration issues
  • Email account troubleshooting and adding devices
  • Security and dependency updates where applicable
  • Help coordinating domain and hosting renewals
  • Guidance by call or message on using the website and email

Minor Changes (free for {{ c.project.free_changes_window_days }} days after Go-Live)

A Minor Change is a small update to existing pages that takes no more than about one hour of work, such as:

  • Editing text, prices, phone numbers or addresses
  • Replacing images or logos in existing places
  • Adjusting colours, spacing or font sizes
  • Updating links and social media icons

After {{ c.project.free_changes_window_days }} days, or for larger work, changes are quoted under Clause 8.
