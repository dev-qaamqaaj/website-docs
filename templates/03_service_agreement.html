What we do

  1. Development (done). We have designed and built the Website ({{ cl.site_pages | join(", ") }}) from the company profile and content {{ cl.business }} provided.
  2. Launch. We put the Website live on your domain and set up everything included in the package you choose (see the Fees & Package page).
  3. Care. With Complete Care, we look after the Website until {{ c.project.support_until }}, and minor changes are free for the first {{ c.project.free_changes_window_days }} days after go-live. With Launch Essentials, we fix any bugs you report within {{ c.project.launch_warranty_days }} days of go-live.
  4. Anything new, such as extra pages, new features or a redesign, is not included. We'll quote it separately, and we only start once you agree.

Fees and payment

  1. The fees are on the Fees & Package page. They are special referral rates, below our standard pricing.
  2. You pay in two instalments: the development fee plus half of the launch fee on signing, and the remaining half on the day the Website goes live.
  3. You can pay by bank transfer, Easypaisa, JazzCash or cash, and we issue a receipt for every payment.
  4. If a payment is more than 7 days late, we may pause work until it's received.

Domain, hosting and email are yours

  1. You buy the domain, hosting and any email plan directly from the provider, in {{ cl.business }}'s name. We help you choose and buy, and we don't charge any commission.
  2. You own these accounts and pay their yearly renewals to the provider. We'll remind you when a renewal is coming up during the care period.
  3. At launch we hand over all logins. You can change passwords or remove our access at any time.

What we need from you

  1. Timely content, feedback and access (for example, the pending photos and logos). If these are delayed, launch may move back.
  2. Confirmation that you have the right to use the text, images and logos you send us.

Ownership of the Website

  1. Once all fees are paid, the Website's design, content and code made for you belong to {{ cl.business }}.
  2. We may show the Website in our portfolio unless you tick here:  Please don't.

Privacy

  1. Both sides keep each other's business information, prices and passwords private.

Fair limits

  1. We do our work carefully and professionally. We can't control outages at the hosting or email provider, but we'll help you resolve them.
  2. Our total responsibility under this agreement is limited to the fees you have paid us.

Taxes

  1. Each side takes care of its own taxes under Pakistani law. If you deduct any withholding tax, please share the certificate with us.

Ending the agreement

  1. Either side can end this agreement with 14 days' written notice (WhatsApp is fine).
  2. Work already completed, including development, is paid for and not refundable. We'll hand over all logins and a copy of the Website once dues are cleared.

If we disagree

  1. We'll sit down and sort it out in good faith. If that doesn't work, this agreement follows the laws of Pakistan and the courts of {{ c.project.city }}.

General

  1. This agreement replaces earlier discussions about this project. Changes are agreed in writing, and a clear WhatsApp or email confirmation from both sides counts.
  2. This agreement can be signed on paper or electronically (Electronic Transactions Ordinance, 2002), and each signed copy counts as an original.
