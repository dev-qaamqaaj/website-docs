This agreement is effective from {{ c.project.issue_date }} and covers the {{ cl.business }} website (the "Website").

Scope of services

  1. Development. OBD has designed and developed the Website ({{ cl.site_pages | join(", ") }}) using the company profile, content, photos and logos supplied by the Client, and will complete the final details before launch.
  2. Deployment. OBD will configure the domain and DNS, set up hosting, install an SSL certificate, publish the Website, and configure one (1) professional mailbox on one phone and one laptop.
  3. Launch support. OBD will fix any defects in its work reported within {{ c.project.launch_warranty_days }} days of go-live, at no charge.
  4. New pages, features, redesigns and ongoing maintenance are outside this scope. OBD will quote them in writing and will begin only after the Client approves the quote.

Fees and payment

  1. The development fee is {{ pr.development.offered | money }} (standard rate {{ pr.development.standard | money }}), payable on signing this agreement.
  2. The deployment fee (standard rate {{ pr.deployment.standard | money }}) is waived in full, as the deployment charges were not communicated clearly to the Client at the outset.
  3. Additional professional email accounts, beyond the one included, are charged at {{ pr.extra_mailbox_fee | money }} per account, payable to OBD at the time of setup. The mailbox subscription for every account is paid by the Client directly to the email provider.
  4. Payment may be made by bank transfer, Easypaisa or JazzCash. OBD will issue a receipt for every payment.

Third-party services and ownership of accounts

  1. The domain, hosting and email subscriptions are purchased by the Client directly from the providers, in the Client's name, with OBD's guidance. OBD charges no commission on these services.
  2. The Client owns these accounts and is responsible for their renewal fees. Indicative costs: {% for t in pr.third_party_estimates %}{{ t.item }} {{ t.estimate }}; {% endfor %}{{ pr.mailbox_provider_note | lower }}.
  3. OBD will hand over all account access at launch. The Client may change passwords or revoke OBD's access at any time.

Client responsibilities

  1. The Client will provide timely feedback, approvals and account access. Delays may affect the launch date.
  2. The Client confirms that it has the right to use all content, images and logos supplied for the Website.

Ownership

  1. On full payment of the fees due, ownership of the Website's design, content and custom code passes to the Client.
  2. OBD may reference the Website in its portfolio unless the Client objects:  The Client does not consent.

Confidentiality

  1. Each party will keep the other's business information, pricing and account credentials confidential.

Liability

  1. OBD will perform the services with reasonable skill and care. OBD is not responsible for outages or changes made by third-party providers, but will assist the Client in resolving them.
  2. OBD's total liability under this agreement is limited to the fees paid by the Client.

Taxes

  1. Each party is responsible for its own taxes under the laws of Pakistan. Where the Client withholds tax, it will provide OBD with the relevant certificate.

Termination

  1. Either party may terminate this agreement with fourteen (14) days' written notice, including by email or WhatsApp.
  2. Fees for work already completed are non-refundable. On termination, OBD will hand over all account access and a copy of the Website once outstanding fees are settled.

Governing law and disputes

  1. The parties will first seek to resolve any dispute amicably. This agreement is governed by the laws of Pakistan, and the courts of {{ c.project.city }} will have jurisdiction.

General

  1. This agreement supersedes all previous discussions about this project. Any changes must be agreed in writing, and written confirmation from both parties by email or WhatsApp is sufficient.
  2. This agreement may be signed on paper or electronically under the Electronic Transactions Ordinance, 2002. Each signed copy is an original.
